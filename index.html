Your identity, kept on your own machine.

MetaMe keeps your identity, credentials and keys in an encrypted vault on your computer. When someone asks for proof, you choose exactly which details to share.

  • No account to create
  • No telemetry
  • Your keys never leave your device

MetaMe is a desktop app. Open this page on your computer to install it.

Early access

MetaMe hasn't had an independent security audit yet, and the installers aren't code-signed. Windows and macOS will warn you the first time you open it. The steps for each system are below.

Version 0.1.10

Before you start

There is no password reset.

Nobody holds a copy of your vault, including us. If you forget your master password and have no backup, no one can bring your identity back. Set up a safety net as soon as you install.

Backup fileA sealed copy of your vault. It restores everything on a new device, opened with your password.
Recovery sharesYour key split into pieces you keep in separate places. They restore your vault even if you forget your password.

Afterwards, open Test recovery shares from Home to check that your shares work. The test changes nothing.

What it does today

As of version 0.1.10

Works now

  • Create your identity and an encrypted vault on your computer
  • Back up with a file or recovery shares, and test recovery safely
  • Hold credentials and share only the details you choose
  • Keep separate Personal and Work profiles
  • Install signed add-on modules from the registry
  • Join an organization's managed Work profile
